## Positioning

Runway Arcade turns Xero-style finance data into an evidence-backed cashflow cockpit for small businesses. It shows cash runway, near-term hazards, overdue invoice recovery opportunities, route plans, approval gates, and an AI co-pilot that explains its recommendations.

The current local app is simulation-only. It does not make real Xero writes, send payment instructions, or contact customers.
