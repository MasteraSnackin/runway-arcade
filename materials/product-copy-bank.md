## Short Descriptions

### 25 Words

Runway Arcade turns Xero-style invoices, bills and bank balances into an evidence-backed cashflow cockpit for small businesses.

### 50 Words

Runway Arcade helps small businesses understand cash runway, spot payment hazards, prioritise overdue invoice recovery and approve safe next actions. It uses Xero-style finance data, deterministic calculations and a bounded co-pilot to keep recommendations explainable, auditable and simulation-only in the MVP.

### 100 Words

Runway Arcade is a Xero finance co-pilot for small businesses. It converts invoices, bills, bank accounts, payments and reports into a cockpit-style cashflow command centre. The dashboard shows runway, due bills, overdue invoices, seven-day forecast, recommended routes, approval queues and an audit trail. The co-pilot can answer cash questions and propose actions, but it does not act alone. The MVP is simulation-only: no real Xero writes, payments or customer chases occur. A production version should keep the same evidence and approval model while replacing demo fixtures with live Xero OAuth reads.

## FAQ Copy

### Does this write to Xero?

No. The current MVP is simulation-only. It demonstrates what an approval workflow could look like but does not write to Xero, send payments or contact customers.

### Is live Xero connected?

Not in the local mirror. The interface shows an adapter-ready path and demo fallback state. A production build would need server-side OAuth, encrypted token storage, exact scopes and rate-limit handling.

### Why the arcade/cockpit metaphor?

Cash pressure is easier to understand when it is operational. Fuel, hazards, rescue beacons and routes map neatly to cash, bills, receivables and payment decisions.

### What makes the AI safe?

The assistant should use bounded tools, deterministic finance calculations, explicit evidence, approval gates and audit logs. It should not independently execute financial actions.

### What is Tiger Mum Mode?

Tiger Mum Mode is an optional stricter co-pilot voice: direct, caring and disciplined about cashflow. It can use the Auntie Mei persona, an Asian-British family-business cashflow mentor, but it must avoid caricature, fake accent, stereotypes or shame. The finance guardrails stay exactly the same.
